Healthy Greek Lentil Soup (Fakes)

Greek • Mediterranean • High fibre • Plant protein

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 45 min · Serves: 6

Ingredients

  • 350 g dry brown or green lentils, rinsed
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 400 g canned chopped tomatoes, no added sugar
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp dried thyme
  • ½ tsp sweet or smoked paprika, optional
  • 1.5 litres water or unsalted vegetable stock
  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, divided
  • 2 tbsp red-wine vinegar, plus more to serve
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • ¼–½ tsp salt, optional
  • Fresh parsley, chopped, to serve
  • Lemon wedges, optional

Instructions

  1. Rinse the lentils well under cold water.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion, carrots and celery. Cook for 6–8 minutes.
  3. Add garlic, tomato paste, oregano, thyme and paprika. Cook 30–60 seconds.
  4. Add lentils, tomatoes, bay leaves and 1.5 litres water or unsalted stock. Bring to a boil.
  5. Reduce to a gentle simmer, partially cover and cook for 35–45 minutes until tender.
  6. Remove bay leaves. Stir in vinegar and the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil. Add black pepper and only a little salt if needed.
  7. Rest for 5–10 minutes. Finish with parsley and optional lemon or extra vinegar.
