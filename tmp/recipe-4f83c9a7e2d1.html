Healthy Greek Lentil Soup (Fakes)

A healthy, traditional-style Greek lentil soup made with plenty of vegetables, modest extra-virgin olive oil, herbs and red-wine vinegar, with no butter, cream or added sugar.

Prep: 15 minutes · Cook: 45 minutes · Serves: 6

Ingredients

  • 350 g dry brown or green lentils, rinsed
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 400 g canned chopped tomatoes, no added sugar
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1/2 teaspoon sweet or smoked paprika, optional
  • 1.5 litres water or unsalted vegetable stock
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
  • 2 tablespoons red-wine vinegar, plus more to serve
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon salt, optional
  • Fresh parsley, chopped, to serve
  • Lemon wedges, optional, to serve

Instructions

  1. Rinse the lentils well under cold running water and remove any debris.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon of the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots and celery and cook for 6 to 8 minutes until softened, stirring often.
  3. Add the garlic, tomato paste, oregano, thyme and paprika. Cook for 30 to 60 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add the lentils, chopped tomatoes, bay leaves and water or unsalted stock. Bring to a boil.
  5. Reduce to a gentle simmer, partially cover and cook for 35 to 45 minutes until the lentils are tender. Add a little extra hot water if the soup becomes too thick.
  6. Remove the bay leaves. Stir in the red-wine vinegar and the remaining tablespoon of extra-virgin olive oil. Season with black pepper and only a little salt if needed.
  7. Rest for 5 to 10 minutes. Serve with chopped parsley and, if desired, a squeeze of lemon or an extra splash of red-wine vinegar.
